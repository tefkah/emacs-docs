slug: Exceptions
---

There are two exceptions to rules governing Shorthand transformations:

- Symbol forms comprised entirely of characters in the Emacs Lisp symbol constituent class (see [Table of Syntax Classes](/docs/elisp/Syntax-Class-Table)) are not transformed. For example, it's possible to use `-` or `/=` as shorthand prefixes, but that won't shadow the arithmetic *functions* of those names.
- Symbol forms whose names start with '`#_`' are not transformed.
